PROJECT PREVIEW

A look at the ATC experience

01 · OVERVIEW

Project Overview

Arora Trading Company (ATC) is a product-focused e-commerce website created around a real business use case. The website provides customers with an organized way to explore automotive batteries, inverters and related products.

The interface is structured around product categories and simple navigation, while also providing customer interaction points such as the shopping cart, WhatsApp contact, location information and a contact form.

Building the Experience

While developing ATC, I focused on keeping the interface simple and easy to navigate. Product categories and product cards were structured so users could quickly understand what the business offers.
